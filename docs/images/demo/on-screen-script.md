# Evidence Lab on-screen script

## Cover

Evidence Lab

The free document intelligence platform

## Search

Getting the basics right with AI-powered search

Evidence Lab is a free, open-source document intelligence platform. It comes with a document processing pipeline and a range of AI tools for retrieving evidence and writing.

It was designed with people to help with their workflows and keep them in the driving seat.

### Find the evidence

The basics are important, so the system includes a powerful search engine you can configure for a range of retrieval algorithms.

An AI summary provides a quick overview.

Search results and citations take you directly to the page, with the relevant content highlighted.

## Research Assistant

Do your research conversationally

Like ChatGPT and Claude, Research Assistant lets you carry out interactive research.

You can keep asking questions and drill down to find what you need.

## Brief

Human-guided deep research

Many platforms have deep research features these days. Brief was designed a little differently to give people more control over the research structure and content.

### From outline to draft

You can generate an outline using your document library. Then have AI research each section.

### Fact Checker

Sections can be verified with an integrated fact checker.

### Review an AI edit

You can ask AI to help you edit a section or update it to include recent documents.

### Collaboration

Evidence Lab comes with collaboration features like comments and sharing.

### Export to Word

Export the finished brief and its citations to Word.

## Heatmapper

Find patterns in your documents

Trends in your document library can reveal useful signals. Heatmapper brings those trends to the surface.

### Track trends in your content

You can build maps at the document level using metadata fields, including AI tags Evidence Lab applies during ingestion.

## Environment

LLM usage and environmental impact

Evidence Lab works with large or small models, open or proprietary. You can track spend and environmental impact.

To help people understand the potential environmental impact, Evidence Lab includes a module to track model usage, tokens, costs and estimated environmental impact.

## Pipeline

Low-cost document processing and AI tagging

Evidence Lab has an integrated pipeline for processing documents and tagging sections so research can focus on key material.

Processing statistics show where to focus on parsing and quality issues.

## Evaluation

In-app AI evaluation

Evaluation is built in, so users can be part of the process. Keeping evaluation in the app makes management and deployment simpler.

### Make sure AI is behaving

Users can rate results, give feedback and help evaluate performance systematically.

## Manage your docs

Use AI to augment document data

Evidence Lab provides visibility into the document library, to include management of AI-generated summaries and tags.

Evidence Lab uses AI-powered tagging to help you find and filter documents.

## Admin

Configure and monitor the system

Configuration can be set for teams, with deep control over search, format and AI outputs.
